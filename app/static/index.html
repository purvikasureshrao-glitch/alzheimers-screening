Four short checks.
One calm, clear picture.

A screening aid that looks at drawing, movement, eyes and speech. It is not a diagnosis; a clinician reviews every result.

Tell us about you

Every box is needed. These answers are saved for your doctor and never change a test result. You will get a patient ID when you continue.

1 · Draw a clock

Draw a clock face with all 12 numbers and set the hands to ten past eleven (11:10). Write the numbers large and clear. A touchscreen or pen works best; a mouse is fine too.

2 · Finger tapping

Use your usual hand. When the countdown ends, tap the big button with one finger as fast as you can for 30 seconds.

Ready

3 · Eye movement

Three short tasks using your webcam. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, in good light, and keep your head still. Each picture stays on screen for 3 seconds. The camera is used only for this test.

4 · Speech

Look at the picture and describe everything that is happening in it, in as much detail as you can. You have one minute.

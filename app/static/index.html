1 · Draw a clock

Using the mouse, draw a clock face with all the numbers, and set the hands to ten past eleven (11:10).

2 · Finger tapping

Use your usual hand. When the countdown ends, tap the big button with one finger as fast as you can for 30 seconds.

3 · Eye movement Experimental · not counted

Three short tasks using your webcam. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, in good light, and keep your head still. The camera is used only for this test.

A webcam records far fewer eye samples than the research trackers this model was trained on, so this result is shown but not counted in the final recommendation.
